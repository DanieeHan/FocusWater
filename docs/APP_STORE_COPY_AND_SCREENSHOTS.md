# FocusWater App Store Copy And Screenshot Script

## Positioning

FocusWater is a focus tracker that turns daily concentration time into bottles of water.

Product angle:

- Gentle motivation
- Visual progress
- Daily goal clarity
- Calm Apple-style experience

## Store copy draft

### App name

- FocusWater

### Subtitle options

- Turn focus into visible progress
- Fill one bottle with every focused day
- A calm visual focus tracker

Recommended:

- Turn focus into visible progress

### Keywords draft

- focus,timer,study,productivity,habit,deep work,pomodoro,tracker

### Short promotional text

- Build focus with a calmer daily rhythm. Track every session, fill each bottle, and see your progress grow over time.

### Description draft

FocusWater turns your focus time into something you can see.

Set a daily goal, log focused minutes, and gradually fill a bottle of water as you work. Every completed bottle becomes part of your archive, giving you a simple and satisfying way to track consistency.

What you can do:

- Set a custom daily focus goal
- Log sessions quickly with presets or custom time
- Use the built-in timer to save real focus directly
- Watch your current bottle fill up as you progress
- Review completed bottles in your archive
- Check weekly trends, streaks, and recent focus history
- Switch between Chinese and English
- Use light mode, dark mode, or follow system appearance

FocusWater is designed to feel calm, visual, and lightweight, so staying focused feels a little more rewarding every day.

## Screenshot strategy

Do not use random live-data screenshots. Prepare intentional demo data first.

Suggested demo profile:

- Daily goal: `6h 30m`
- Current bottle progress around `55% to 70%`
- At least `6 to 10` completed bottles in archive
- Recent sessions with meaningful notes
- Weekly chart with visible variation
- Heatmap with mixed intensity
- Streak greater than `5`
